About

Dr. Mukesh Delu received the M.Sc. degree in Mathematics from the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Kanpur in 2021 and the Ph.D. degree from the Department of Mathematics, Malaviya National Institute of Technology (MNIT) Jaipur, in 2026. Dr. Delu is currently an Assistant Professor of Mathematics in the Department of College Education, Government of Rajasthan, and a lifetime member of the Ramanujan Mathematical Society (RMS).

The research of Dr. Delu sits where fractional calculus meets medical image analysis: designing fractional-order kernels, filters and optimisers, and using them inside deep networks that read retinal fundus images. This doctoral work on fractional-order kernels for retinal image analysis forms the mathematical basis of ChakshuRakshak AI, a diabetic retinopathy screening system now in clinical validation at SMS Hospital, Jaipur.

Institute

Supervisor
Dr. Priyanka Harjule

Fractional-order filtering

Fractional Frangi filters, fractional Hessian curvature analysis and compass-gradient edge masks for enhancing and segmenting retinal vasculature.

Deep networks for grading

Dual-path and fractional-operator architectures for diabetic retinopathy detection, staging and macular edema grading.

Fractional optimisation

Training deep networks with fractional-order gradient descent to improve convergence and screening accuracy.

Datasets & benchmarks

Harmonised multi-source fundus datasets with baseline deep-learning validation for reproducible DR staging.

ChakshuRakshak AI

AI-based retinal analysis from fundus images — diabetic retinopathy grading, diabetic macular edema grading and a multi-disease screen. In clinical validation at SMS Hospital, Jaipur.
